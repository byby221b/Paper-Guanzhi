# ICCV — Helmholtz Prize

> **Official pages:** [CVF](https://www.thecvf.com/?page_id=537) | [IEEE TC-PAMI](https://tc.computer.org/tcpami/awards/the-helmholtz-prize/)
>
> Formally named **Helmholtz Prize** since 2013. The 2013 inaugural edition was a retrospective covering all ICCV papers from 1987–1999. ICCV is biennial (odd years only).

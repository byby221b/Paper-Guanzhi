# Foundations

> Papers selected not by any award committee, but by their undeniable place in the history of AI.
> Primary source: Nils Nilsson, [*The Quest for Artificial Intelligence*](https://ai.stanford.edu/~nilsson/QAI/qai.pdf) (Cambridge University Press, 2010), cross-referenced with [Ilya Sutskever's recommended reading list](https://github.com/dzyim/ilya-sutskever-recommended-reading) and other authoritative sources.

**Selection criteria:**

- Introduced an entirely new paradigm or methodology
- Still widely cited and taught decades after publication
- Had irreversible, structural impact on the field
- Recognized as a classic by multiple independent authoritative sources
